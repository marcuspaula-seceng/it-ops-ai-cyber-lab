# Meeting Room Readiness Checklist (Generic)

A generic readiness checklist for a meeting room supporting Lark and Zoom.
Contains no real room, floor or building names.

## 1. Scheduling and control

- [ ] Room scheduler / panel online and showing the correct room.
- [ ] Room controller responsive.
- [ ] Correct date, time and calendar shown.

## 2. Display

- [ ] Primary display / projector powers on and shows a signal.
- [ ] Correct resolution and aspect ratio.
- [ ] HDMI input(s) working from the table connection.

## 3. Camera

- [ ] Camera detected and produces a clear image.
- [ ] Framing and pan/tilt/zoom (where present) work.

## 4. Audio

- [ ] Microphone(s) capture clearly across the room.
- [ ] Speakers produce clear audio at usable volume.
- [ ] No noticeable echo or feedback.

## 5. Conferencing platforms

- [ ] Lark meeting starts and connects.
- [ ] Zoom meeting starts and connects.
- [ ] Switching between Lark and Zoom works.
- [ ] Screen sharing over wireless works.
- [ ] Screen sharing over HDMI works.

## 6. Overall result

- [ ] Result recorded as **pass**, **warning** or **fail**.
- [ ] Any issue documented with the fix applied.

> The demonstration reporting tool aggregates the pass / warning / fail results
> from a CSV of rooms and reports the totals and readiness rate.
